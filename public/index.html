How to play

Take turns placing cards on the 3×3 board. When your card's side beats the touching side of an opponent's card, that card flips to your colour. When the board is full, whoever owns more cards (counting the one left in hand) wins.

Same: if 2+ sides you touch have equal numbers, those cards flip. Plus: if 2+ touching pairs add to the same total, those cards flip. Cards flipped by Same or Plus can chain-capture (Combo). Elemental: marked squares give +1 to a matching element and −1 to everything else. Open: both hands are visible.
