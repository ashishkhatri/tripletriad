    How to play

    The basics

    Take turns placing cards on the 3×3 board. Each card has a number on every side (A is 10). When your card's side is higher than the touching side of an opponent's card, that card flips to your colour. When the board is full, whoever owns more cards — counting the one left in hand — wins.

    Reading a card

    The border shows rarity: bronze (common), silver (uncommon), gold (rare), diamond (epic) and prismatic (legendary). The icon in the top corner is the card's element. On the board, a blue name plate is yours and red is your opponent's. Shimmering holographic cards are foils.

    Rules

    Pick extra rules under Match rules on the Play screen — rest your pointer on (or tap) each i to see what it does.

    Collecting

    Signed-in players earn XP from every game: 10 for playing, 25 more for a win. Every 100 XP earns a card pack, which you can open from any set. Scrap cards you don't need for shards, and spend shards to craft the ones you want. Three daily challenges pay extra shards.
